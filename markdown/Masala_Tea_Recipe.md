# Masala Tea Recipe

## My dialed down recipe

**Mitanshu Sukhwani** • _07 June 2025_

# Ingredients:

1. 5 gms [Taj Mahal Tea](https://amzn.in/d/fNYPyQp)

2. 1.8 gms [Sugar Free stevia](https://www.sugarfree-india.com/sweeteners/sugar-free-green) OR 9 gms [madhur normal sugar](https://madhursugar.com/madhur-sugar)

3. 1 gm [Everest Tea Masala](https://www.everestfoods.com/products/tea-masala/)

4. 40 gms freshly boiled water, preferrably from [Aquaguard](https://www.croma.com/eureka-forbes-aquaguard-6-litres-water-purifier-geneus-xpert-white-/p/189782).

5. 100 gms [Full Cream Milk (6% fat minimum)](https://www.amul.com/products/amul-gold-info.php).

# Instructions:

1. Mix tea, sugar, and masala in a double wall insulated steel mug with water. Steep for 4 minutes.

2. Transfer the mix in a vessel you will boil the milk in.

3. Add milk in the mug, swirl, and transfer the milk into the vessel.

4. Keep gas on full flame and bring to boil once, till a certain amber color is achieved. If not, try edging.

5. Strain and enjoy!

# Thoughts

1. I believe it scales linearly for 2-3 times the output size, beyond which I have never had to make tea.

2. Tastes best with normal sugar :)
